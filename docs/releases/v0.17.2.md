# Autolith 0.17.2

Autolith 0.17.2 dresses the launcher and bootstrap flow in the
interface's own look: brand-colored section headings, detail lines under
the familiar dim vertical rail, and a green completion line.

## Styled bootstrap and launcher

- Rendered every bootstrap step as a brand-styled section whose detail
  lines flow under the interface's dim vertical rail, including output
  from the dependency, recovery-image, and fast-startup-image
  subprocesses.
- Collapsed carriage-return progress redraws into their final text, so
  dependency loading no longer garbles the transcript.
- Moved locked-dependency materialization into a dedicated railed
  subprocess, so threaded and grandchild writers land under the rail
  too.
- Colored the launcher's bootstrap offer, failure, and fallback
  messages, honoring NO_COLOR and non-interactive output everywhere.
